# Pricing & Call-In Analysis

**Focus:** Business analytics · Python · Jupyter Notebook · SQL

## Business problem
Pricing, invoice, and incoming-inquiry information can be difficult to compare when spread across operational sources. Analysis needs consistent definitions and repeatable steps.

## Solution approach
The private analysis repository includes notebooks related to commercial and residential pricing, individual and pricing-related call-ins, and invoice totals. The public case study highlights the analytical workflow—framing questions, preparing tabular data, comparing segments, and documenting findings—without publishing the underlying records.

## Skills represented
Data preparation, SQL and notebook-based exploration, segmentation, and communicating limitations of operational data.

**Disclosure:** Original customer tables, invoices, database dumps, raw CSVs, business-specific figures, and notebook outputs remain private. No financial impact or causal benefit is claimed without independently shareable evidence. A future open example should use synthetic data created specifically for the demo.
