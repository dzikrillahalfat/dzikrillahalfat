# Lead Discovery & Follow-Up Tool

**Focus:** Sales research workflow · Node.js · JavaScript · SQLite

## Business problem
Sales prospect research can involve switching between sources, spreadsheets, and messaging tools. A structured workflow can make the follow-up status more visible.

## Solution approach
The private project documentation describes a browser-accessible tool for researching business listings, recording potential leads, assigning follow-up status, and exporting worklists. The case study focuses on workflow structure rather than publishing scraped contacts or the retrieval implementation.

## Skills represented
Thinking through search-to-follow-up workflows, multi-user record ownership, practical filtering, and lightweight reporting.

**Disclosure:** No collected contact details, prospect lists, scraping code, credentials, or private company data are published. Any future public demo must use fictional data and respect relevant source terms and privacy requirements. This page is not an endorsement of unrestricted scraping or mass messaging.
