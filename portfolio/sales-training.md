# Sales Training Needs Questionnaire

**Focus:** Requirements gathering · Python · SQLite · Web forms

## Business problem
Training plans are harder to prioritize when feedback arrives in unstructured messages or disconnected spreadsheets.

## Solution approach
The private project documentation describes a responsive web questionnaire for collecting training needs, capturing structured responses, and reviewing them through an administrative dashboard with filtering and export. The design also includes editable questions.

## Skills represented
Translating survey requirements into validated inputs, designing an accessible collection workflow, structuring response storage, and making results easier to review.

**Disclosure:** Real responses, signatures, personal identifiers, authentication details, and internal documents are not shared. This overview does not imply verified changes to employee performance.
