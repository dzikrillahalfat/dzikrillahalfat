# Grooming Knowledge Quiz

**Focus:** Employee learning workflows · PHP · Browser-based assessments

## Business problem
Repeated knowledge checks can be difficult to administer consistently when questions, participant identities, and scores are recorded manually.

## Solution approach
The private project documents participant registration, scheduled quiz periods, varied question assignments, scores linked to participant accounts, and an admin view for reviewing results and exporting records. This case study describes the system design without exposing the original quiz materials or participant data.

## Skills represented
Designing assessment rules, account-based workflows, reproducible assignments, basic administrative reporting, and structured exports.

**Disclosure:** No participant names, identifiers, passwords, answer sets, private artwork, or actual results are published. This page is not a claim of verified training outcomes or a live demo.
