# CRM & Lead Routing

**Focus:** Business process design · PHP · SQLite · AI-assisted classification

## Business problem
New customer inquiries need a consistent journey from intake to qualification, assignment, and follow-up. Manual hand-offs can make responsibility and reporting harder to track.

## Solution approach
The private project documentation describes a CRM workflow combining lead records, location- and category-based routing rules, a human-managed inbox, and optional AI-supported message classification. Routing fallbacks and manual takeover are part of the documented design.

## Skills represented
Mapping a sales and customer-service process, separating automated recommendations from human decisions, modeling lead statuses, and thinking about access controls and reporting needs.
